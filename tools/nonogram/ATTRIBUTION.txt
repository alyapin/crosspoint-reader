The random nonograms (lib/Games/Nonogram.cpp, generate()) follow the puzzle
generator of Pattern from Simon Tatham's Portable Puzzle Collection
(https://www.chiark.greenend.org.uk/~sgtatham/puzzles/): random noise smoothed
over each 3x3 neighbourhood, cut at the median, and rerolled until a line
solver proves the answer unique. The collection is distributed under the MIT
